# ROG5 stock vs ours: ASUS-specific drivers, hooks and board setup

Scope: everything ASUS added on top of the QTI msm-5.4 tree that has no counterpart in our 7.2.7 build (r42). Ground truth for "what stock actually runs" is the flashed MP5 overlay (`~/.local/state/rog5-stock-payload-20260927/dtbo/dtbo17.dtb`, decompiled to scratchpad `kernel-compare/asus/dtbo17.dts`; MP/MP3/MP4/ANAKIN2-MP differ by 8 lines = model/board-id only). The overlay sources (`arch/arm64/boot/dts/vendor/qcom/ZS673KS-*-overlay.dts`, chain MP5→MP4→MP3→MP→PR1→ER2→ER1→SR1→EVB2→EVB) are older than the flashed dtbo: the dtbo adds `hw_stage = 5` for the Aura MCU (dtbo17:11257), the USB2 redriver fragment (11262-11270) and the NFC VEN move to tlmm66. Phone was unreachable (both addresses timed out), so everything is from source. Bottom port / USB3803 hub / RT1711 / redriver, Wi-Fi antenna GPIOs + L7E, thermistors/skin policy, and PMI_MUX_EN/POGO_OTG_EN charger GPIOs are in the connectivity, cpu-thermal and (pending) battery reports; only referenced here.

## 2. Details for the top items

**1. Accessory detection and the cooler.** The whole "ROG accessory" stack is: PMIC ADC identifies what sits on the pogo/side pins → `ec_hid` flips POGO_DET (tlmm166) and, for the backcover, the USB2 mux (tlmm45) plus pm8350 L2 → init.asus.rc switches the USB3803 into hub mode and the bottom-port dwc3 into `mode_POGO inbox` → the cooler's EC enumerates as a Realtek-VID HID device → `rog5_inbox` exposes `/sys/class/leds/aura_inbox/*` (RGB, `fan_enable`, `fan_rpm`) and thermal-engine steps the fan from `virtual-therm`. Nothing in this chain needs a vendor kernel driver on Linux: ADC via IIO, GPIOs via gpiod, the EC via hidraw. Our DT already has the `spmi-adc7` node; the AMUX_THM4 channel and the two TLMM lines must be added. All of it hangs on the bottom-port controller (usb1/a800000) that connectivity reports as dead, so it is a follow-up to that item. Idle state is identical to stock (both GPIOs low, L2 off), so there is no standby cost from the missing driver. Also worth knowing: the `asus_pogo_vadc` ADC-TM thresholds (`ec_hid_driver.c:480-505`) give an interrupt-driven insert/remove path; polling once a second from userspace is fine.

**2. NFC.** This is the only ASUS-managed line whose power-on state we neither know nor control. The stock driver's first act is VEN low; ours never touches tlmm66. A PN553 with VEN high and no host sits in its own idle mode and asserts CLK_REQ when it wants the 19.2 MHz reference, which on this board goes to a PMIC clock buffer (tlmm63, `qcom,sn-clkreq`). If the buffer stays requested, the XO cannot shut down. This is speculative until the GPIO level is read, but it is a 30-second check and a one-line hog to fix, and it would fit the "standby draws 79 mA, no CX/DDR collapse" picture.

**4. AirTriggers.** Everything about it is vendor-only (Sentons firmware, register protocol, a 3 k-line event engine). It is off in our build: the 2.8/1.2 V rails come from the two PM8008 camera PMICs, which we do not declare, and the reset (PM8350C GPIO4) idles low. Nothing to fix for power; note it as the one ROG-specific input that will not come back without a large port.

**7. Haptics reset.** Small but real: stock added tlmm116 as a reset in ER1 and pulses it at probe. Our node omits it and works only because the line is already high. Declaring it costs nothing and makes cold boots deterministic.

**8. Rails.** ASUS adds only three regulator decisions of its own: L8C always-on (we match), L7C 3.3 V for the P-sensor (ours: left on by the boot chain, so no delta vs. stock), and S10B/S12B settings for the grip sensor (our S12 handling in patch 0036 is for CNSS and unrelated). Everything else ASUS powers (pm8008 rails for AirTriggers/FP/redriver, pm8350 L2 for the backcover mux, L9C for back touch, L11C for the antenna tuner) is enabled on demand by a driver we do not run, i.e. it stays off. I found no ASUS GPIO that stock parks in a low-power state and we leave driven: POGO_DET/USB2_MUX2_EN/SNT reset/PMOLED PWREN are all "low unless a driver raises them"; the Wi-Fi antenna lines are output-high in both.

## 3. Checked, same or not needed

- Aura logo MCU: same power model (GPIO2 only, hw_stage 5), same I2C address; LGF ID pin unused in both at runtime.
- `ms51_backcover` (@0x16 on SE15) and `rog6_nuc1261_ms51_inbox` (0x0416:0x3F00/3F10, Cooler 6): accessory-only, same path as item 1.
- Wi-Fi antenna switch tlmm141/142/144 and `wlan-ant-switch-supply` L7E: covered in connectivity (#4); ours matches the pinctrl.
- USB2 redriver (tlmm3/62, pm8008j L6), USB3803 (tlmm97/98/173), RT1711 (tlmm118), `PMI_MUX_EN` pm8350 GPIO2, `POGO_OTG_EN` PM8350C GPIO3: connectivity / battery reports.
- ASUS thermistors (pa_therm1/2, usb_btmconn/sideconn, deleted TOF/WLC/cam zones) and the `virtual-therm` skin policy: cpu-thermal report #2.
- `gpio-keys` (vol-up PM8350 GPIO6 active-low, wakeup): identical in ours (ours.dts:77).
- ASUS hooks in `extcon.c` (deferred state sync until boot complete), `base/class.c`, `firmware_loader` (haptic firmware path), `spi-msm-geni.c`, `ufshcd.c` sysfs, `socinfo.c` subtype, `msm_venc.c`, `drm_anakin.c` (HDR/FOD sysfs), `traps.c` export: Android-framework glue, nothing to port.
- Tuxera exFAT/NTFS: mainline exfat/ntfs3 cover it.
- `CONFIG_ASUS_GLOBAL_VAR`, `CONFIG_TOUCHSCREEN_ROG` (FTS3658U, ours has its own tool), `CONFIG_GOODIX_FINGERPRINT`: the only ASUS Kconfig symbols in the shipped defconfig (`arch/arm64/configs/vendor/ZS673KS-perf_defconfig`); aura_sync, ec_hid and gamepad are `obj-y`.
- ICM206xx IMU, VL53L5 ToF, ASH sensor hub sources are built but unused on this board (no DT nodes); sensors run on the SLPI (ours: working).
- `asus_debug_region@9b800000` is not RMTFS in stock (stock RMTFS is `qcom,rmtfs_sharedmem` at 0x0 dynamic); our reuse of the region for ramoops is safe.
