# Exact OEM S12 voltage diagnostic

Question: can an exact1350mV request complete after the proved1224mV hold,
with stable UFS/NCM and no radio activation? This tests the request contract,
not physical voltage accuracy or the cause of the earlier1352mV reset.

## Implementation and boundaries

The qualified readback kernel and initramfs are unchanged. The same minimal
two-node diagnostic DT now permits1224–1360mV, matching the retained all60
stock-composition range. Its initial cached voltage remains1224mV; no boot-on,
always-on, GPIO, PCIe, radio or unrelated-node change is added.

`held-oem` performs the proved low-voltage enable/hold, reads fresh S12 state,
then submits exactly one fixed RPMh command: address0x40100, data1350. It cannot
round to1352 or select arbitrary resources/voltages. A new readback must return
the requested lower voltage field, enable1 and AUTO6. Other response bits remain
visible and uninterpreted. Failed reads/writes stop the sequence, and every
successful hold remains pinned even if a later check fails.

This raw diagnostic intentionally bypasses the regulator voltage cache.
`cache_not_updated=1` is explicit: no radio or further ordinary S12 consumer
operation is allowed; preserve the hold and reboot after capture. This is not
the production Wi-Fi voltage implementation. Original write-timeout behavior
and its limitations are unchanged.

testing that exact request; it does not prove the retained source is identical
to the installed stock binary or that a control vote is a physical measurement.

topology/boot, safe battery/temperature, all117 nodes read-only and an armed
