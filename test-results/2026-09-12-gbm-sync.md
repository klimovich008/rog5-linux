# GBM pixels across native-fence contexts, 2026-09-12

Added `--gbm-sync-fd=N` to combine the existing GBM allocation and native-fence
paths. The producer renders its linear BO and exports a fence. A separate,
unshared GLES context imports that fence, queues a server wait, imports the BO
as its own texture/FBO, checks a completion fence, and verifies all 64 channels.
Consumer GL/image resources are destroyed while its context is current, then
the producer is restored and checked. This mode has no `glFinish` fallback.
It preserves the explicit-descriptor, DRM-version and external admission guards.
