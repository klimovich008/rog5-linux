# GPT-6.1-Sol: HDMI PCON at HBR2 (2026-09-30)

Brief: [2026-09-30-dp-pcon-hbr2-brief.md](2026-09-30-dp-pcon-hbr2-brief.md). Codex CLI 0.159.1, gpt-6.1-sol, reasoning high, read-only sandbox. Links point at local paths on the development host.

The strongest concrete findings are **missing sink power sequencing and swallowed training/stream errors**. The sources do **not** support “HBR2 requires additional HDMI 2.0/FRL setup” for this hub.

I reviewed the requested patches, notes, relevant MSM DP/DPU paths, QMP PHY and Type-C routing, DRM helpers, i915, amdgpu DC, and stock Qualcomm implementations. No files were changed and nothing was run against the phone. The proposed diffs below were checked against r93 source contexts in memory; they have not been compiled or hardware tested.

There is enough evidence to propose decisive fixes and experiments, but not enough to claim a proven HBR2 solution.

**Ranked causes**

| Rank | Finding | Evidence and interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Incomplete sink power sequencing** | An ordinary modeset with the hub attached takes `msm_dp_ctrl_off()`, which shuts down the source without putting the sink into D3. The subsequent enable initializes the PHY and programs PCON controls without first requesting D0. Stock explicitly cycles D3→D0 during panel initialization. This is the strongest candidate for the failure to recover and a plausible contributor to rate changes. |
| 2 | **The driver reports success despite failures during stream enable** | Fresh enable trains twice. The second training result is discarded, training-pattern writes are unchecked, and a main-link-ready timeout still returns success. Atomic enable also ignores the stream-enable return value. Therefore “training passed” does not establish that the final stream transition succeeded. |
| 3 | **An HBR2 stream-generation or clock-state defect remains possible** | Successful CR/EQ/symbol lock proves training, not correct video transport. Framebuffer contents prove memory contents, not the transmitted stream. The TPG failure strengthens suspicion of the common DP controller/PHY/converter path. However, the inspected MVID/NVID arithmetic and HBR2 PLL constants do not reveal the proposed obvious divider error. |
| 4 | **PHY state or electrical margin, particularly around enable** | Initial errors could arise during the training-to-video transition. Later quiet counters weaken persistent signal-integrity failure, provided the reads and counters are valid. PHY configuration, PHY power-on and OPP errors are swallowed; the PHY driver itself discards PLL/PHY configuration failures. |
| 5 | **Converter HDMI-side state or firmware behaviour** | Still possible, particularly after a bad stream. But the advertised capabilities and existing control writes argue against missing FRL/source-control programming. D3→D0 is a useful recovery experiment, not a guaranteed converter firmware reset. |
| 6 | **DPU bandwidth, fetch format or performance votes** | Lower priority given the latest zero-underrun observations and failed DP TPG. The DPU patches address separate observed problems, but none establishes the cause of the present HBR2 failure. |

**The HDMI/FRL hypothesis needs correction**

The downstream bytes decode as follows, using [drm_dp.h:400](/home/deck/.local/state/rog5-kernel-7.2.7-build-r93/source/include/drm/display/drm_dp.h:400):

| Byte | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `0x80=0x0b` | HDMI downstream port, HPD aware |
| `0x81=0xf0` | Maximum TMDS clock: `240 × 2.5 MHz = 600 MHz` |
| `0x82=0x02` | Maximum 12 bpc; **FRL bandwidth field zero; source-control capability clear** |
| `0x83=0x1f` | Several YCbCr passthrough/conversion capabilities; RGB→YCbCr conversion bits clear |

HBR2 does not determine the HDMI TMDS clock. The selected pixel clock, encoding and component depth do:

| RGB mode | DP payload | HDMI TMDS character clock |
|---|---:|---:|
| 1080p60, 8 bpc | 3.564 Gb/s | 148.500 MHz |
| 1080p60, 10 bpc | 4.455 Gb/s | 185.625 MHz |
| 3840×1080p60, 8 bpc | 6.840 Gb/s | 285.000 MHz |
| 3840×1080p60, 10 bpc | 8.550 Gb/s | 356.250 MHz |

Thus three failing cases remain below 340 MHz. Only the last crosses the high-rate scrambling boundary. HBR2×2 supplies 8.64 Gb/s before additional transport requirements; the last case also has very little bandwidth margin.

Two statements in the brief are incorrect:

1. **0094 already writes all three converter controls:** `3050=1`, `3051=0`, `3052=0`. See [dp_panel.c:348](/home/deck/.local/state/rog5-kernel-7.2.7-build-r93/source/drivers/gpu/drm/msm/dp/dp_panel.c:348).
2. **`3051=0` leaves EDID processing and scrambling autonomous.** It clears `EDID_PROCESSING_DISABLE` and `AUTONOMOUS_SCRAMBLING_DISABLE`; it does not omit or disable autonomous HDMI scrambling. See [drm_dp.h:1351](/home/deck/.local/state/rog5-kernel-7.2.7-build-r93/source/include/drm/display/drm_dp.h:1351).

`3050` bit 0 selects HDMI versus DVI output. It does not select a TMDS speed limit. Its value is correct for this HDMI monitor.

The identical `3036..303b` snapshots are not decisive. `3036` describes TMDS versus FRL and trained FRL bandwidth; zero is consistent with TMDS. Since the working HBR case also reads `303b=0`, that bit cannot presently serve as a reliable success gate for this converter.

Finally, “replugged **and** rebooted” cannot locate the persistent state exclusively inside the PCON. That reset both the hub-side connection and the phone’s controller/PHY state.

**What the reference drivers write**

For this hub’s **RGB, uncompressed, autonomous TMDS path**, i915’s converter setup is effectively the same as yours:

- `3050`: HDMI/DVI selection.
- `3051`: YCbCr444→420 conversion selection, zero for RGB.
- `3052`: read-modify-write RGB→YCbCr conversion field, zero for RGB.

See [intel_dp_configure_protocol_converter:4513](/home/deck/.local/state/rog5-kernel-7.2.7-build-r93/source/drivers/gpu/drm/i915/display/intel_dp.c:4513). The difference is checked/diagnosed transactions and narrower field ownership, not an additional compulsory HDMI 2.0 enable register.

The additional converter writes found in the reference paths are conditional:

| Path | Writes absent from your path | Applicability |
|---|---|---|
| i915 source-controlled FRL preparation | `305A=0x28`, then maximum-FRL-bandwidth configuration; `305B=bandwidth mask`; finally set `305A` bit 7 | Requires source-control support and an HDMI 2.1 FRL sink. This hub fails the capability gate. |
| i915 TMDS fallback from that path | `305A=0x08`, then `0x88` | Reached through the same source-control/HDMI 2.1 gate; not generic HDMI 2.0 initialization. |
| i915 FRL recovery | Read `305A`, clear bit 7, retrain FRL or select TMDS | Conditional on an already trained FRL link. |
| PCON DSC helpers | PPS parameters at `3180/3182/3184`, or a 128-byte buffer at `3100`; DSC/PPS fields in `3052` | Not required for the tested uncompressed RGB modes. |
| Colour conversion | Conversion bits in `3051/3052` | Conditional on selected stream/sink formats and advertised conversion support. |
| Receiver lifecycle | `600=D0/D3` | Relevant difference in when these writes occur. |
| Source identification | Source OUI/device identity beginning at `300`; AMD also has conditional cable-identification writes | Potential vendor-quirk inputs, not evidence of a missing generic HDMI enable. |

The i915 capability gate is at [intel_dp.c:4393](/home/deck/.local/state/rog5-kernel-7.2.7-build-r93/source/drivers/gpu/drm/i915/display/intel_dp.c:4393), FRL sequence at [4284](/home/deck/.local/state/rog5-kernel-7.2.7-build-r93/source/drivers/gpu/drm/i915/display/intel_dp.c:4284), and TMDS fallback at [4363](/home/deck/.local/state/rog5-kernel-7.2.7-build-r93/source/drivers/gpu/drm/i915/display/intel_dp.c:4363). The [DRM helper documentation](https://kernel.org/doc/html/latest/gpu/drm-kms-helpers.html#c.drm_dp_pcon_frl_prepare) likewise describes these helpers as FRL configuration. `drm_dp_pcon_reset_frl_config()` resets FRL configuration; it is not a universal PCON hardware reset.

In the inspected amdgpu DC tree, I found **no writes to `3050/3051/3052/305A/305B`**. Its active-converter capability path reads downstream capabilities and post-FRL status. The assertion in 0094’s comment that both i915 and amdgpu perform this same setup is unsupported by this source tree.

AMD does perform receiver power sequencing; its mode-switch code explicitly discusses D0/D3 losing source-specific AUX settings: [link_dpms.c:2099](/home/deck/.local/state/rog5-kernel-7.2.7-build-r93/source/drivers/gpu/drm/amd/display/dc/link/link_dpms.c:2099).

AMD’s **native HDMI** path additionally writes SCDC at I²C address `0x54`:

- Source version `02=01`.
- TMDS configuration `20=03` above 340 MHz, `01` for optional low-rate scrambling, otherwise `00`.
- Conditional FRL cleanup: configuration `31=00` and write-one-to-clear update `10=20`.

Those calls belong to the native TMDS transmitter path, gated on HDMI signal type: [link_dpms.c:1889](/home/deck/.local/state/rog5-kernel-7.2.7-build-r93/source/drivers/gpu/drm/amd/display/dc/link/link_dpms.c:1889), [link_ddc.c:549](/home/deck/.local/state/rog5-kernel-7.2.7-build-r93/source/drivers/gpu/drm/amd/display/dc/link/protocols/link_ddc.c:549). Copying them onto a DP source with an autonomous converter risks changing the receiver without matching the converter transmitter.

**The DPCD sequence I would test first**

After orderly stream shutdown, with AUX available and before new training:

| Register | Value | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| `600` | Preserve unrelated bits, power field `03` | Optional diagnostic D3 cycle |
| — | Wait ≥200 μs | Stock propagation delay |
| `600` | Preserve unrelated bits, power field `01` | D0 on every fresh external-DP enable; retry/check |
| — | Wait 1–2 ms after each D0 attempt | Receiver wake |
| `3050` | HDMI bit set; normally `01` | Restore HDMI output after wake |
| `3051` | Relevant low four bits clear; normally `00` | No conversion; autonomous EDID processing/scrambling |
| `3052` | Conversion/DSC/PPS fields clear; normally `00` | Explicit uncompressed RGB path |
| `101` | `82` | Two lanes, enhanced framing |
| `100` | `14` | HBR2 |
| `107/108` | `00 01` for the stock-equivalence test | No downspread advertisement, ANSI 8b/10b |
| `102` | `21`, then `23` | TPS1, then TPS3; scrambling disabled during training |
| `103/104` | Negotiated lane drive settings | Match actual PHY swing/pre-emphasis |
| `102` | `00`, checked | End training before video |

Most link-configuration writes already exist. The additions are correctly timed power writes and error handling. **Do not add `305A/305B` or manual SCDC writes in this first experiment.**

**Recovery without unplugging**

For a reachable hub, the recovery experiment should be:

1. Cancel snooping/CRC activity and disable audio/video. Push idle only while link registers are clocked.
2. End training if necessary and perform the existing source controller/PHY shutdown.
3. Reinitialize the source’s AUX-capable PHY state.
4. Request sink D3, wait ≥200 μs; request D0 with checked retries and a 1–2 ms wake delay.
5. Restore `3050/3051/3052`, reread downstream capabilities/EDID if changed, then train **HBR 1080p60 RGB8** and enable video.

The optional power-cycle diff implements the sink-cycle portion before fresh enable. It cannot force an HDMI HPD transition or guarantee a reset of the converter MCU/HDMI transmitter.

If AUX remains responsive but that sequence fails, distinguish source reset, HDMI-side reconnect, and whole-hub reconnect as separate experiments. There is no justified universal legacy-PCON “hard reset” at `305A`, and no basis here for inventing vendor register writes.

**Five-step test plan**

1. **Establish a checked HBR baseline.**  
   Use 1080p60 RGB8, known-working widebus settings, CRC off, and the error-checking changes. Record actual link rate, depth, `102`, `600`, `3050..3052`, main-link-ready and AUX results. Repeat three independent enables.  
   **Pass:** visible picture and at least 300 clean frames per enable. A strict PCON-read failure means unsupported register behaviour must be resolved before interpreting further tests.

2. **Change only DP rate to HBR2.**  
   Keep the exact 1080p60 RGB8 timing, encoding, orientation and converter values. Start from a known-good recovered state.  
   **Pass:** visible image at actual `540000`, two lanes, 24 bpp. HBR fallback is not a pass. Capture both training passes and the checked transition to video, not merely the first success.

3. **Separate wake, power-cycle and double-training effects.**  
   Compare D0-only against `dp_pcon_power_cycle=1`, keeping two-pass training. Then compare checked two-pass against the single-pass diagnostic, keeping power handling fixed. After each failure, attempt HBR recovery without reboot or unplug.  
   **Pass:** identify a repeatable change that restores HBR2 output or HBR recovery. If only recovery improves, report that separately from fixing HBR2.

4. **If still dark, isolate source transport from DPU and compare stock settings.**  
   Use RGB8 TPG with widebus off, then a DPU stream. Separately test `dp_ssc=0`, stock main-link levels, and corrected polarity. Capture actual pixel/link clock rates, DISPCC M/N, MVID/NVID, TU, MISC, main-link control and PHY registers while powered. Read error counters with successful-transfer and validity information.  
   **Pass:** TPG visible at HBR2 establishes usable source-to-HDMI transport. TPG and DPU both dark with verified stream state directs investigation toward controller/PHY/converter interaction.

5. **Expand modes and capture the Steam Deck’s negotiated path.**  
   First test 3840×1080p60 RGB8 and 1080p120 RGB8. Only then test a mode/depth crossing 340 MHz, with SCDC observation. Capture Steam Deck DP rate/lanes/depth/encoding, converter controls and power sequencing for the same timing.  
   **Pass:** repeated visible output at the intended settings, plus recovery after modesets. Do not treat “Steam Deck works” as a configuration comparison until its actual settings are known.
