## Answer

I would not sign off **4717d357** yet. The review found remaining lock-screen privacy and display-transition problems. No files were modified or objects fetched. The worktree advanced to `651eef1b` during review; findings below target `4717d357`, with newer fixes identified explicitly. Findings are based on source inspection and in-memory probes, without phone testing.

1. **High — Quick settings exposes notifications that lock-screen policy hides.**  
   [quickSettings.js:792](~/.local/state/rog5-gnome-mobile-src/gnome-shell/js/ui/quickSettings.js:792) creates a normal `CalendarMessageList`, without `isOnLockscreen`. Privacy enforcement in [messageList.js:1480](~/.local/state/rog5-gnome-mobile-src/gnome-shell/js/ui/messageList.js:1480) consequently returns immediately. Meanwhile, `sessionMode.js:69–81` retains quick settings while locked, and `panel.js:497–513` permits its swipe gesture. This exposes notifications or bodies whose `showInLockScreen`/`detailsInLockScreen` policy forbids disclosure.  
   **Fix:** Hide the normal list throughout locking and locked states, or use a separate list with lock-screen privacy enforcement. Test both privacy settings through panel taps and swipes, including on DP.

2. **High — Patch 0006 leaves notification URLs independently actionable.**  
   [messageList.js:75](~/.local/state/rog5-gnome-mobile-src/gnome-shell/js/ui/messageList.js:75) launches a URI directly from `URLHighlighter`’s own click gesture. This bypasses the guards added to notification activation and action buttons at lines 877 and 911. Plain URLs are detected automatically; malicious markup is unnecessary. A locked-phone tap can therefore launch an application or URI handler. This is an activation bypass, not evidence of successful session unlocking.  
   **Fix:** Guard URL recognition and activation against both `Main.screenShield.locked` and `Main.sessionMode.isLocked`. Apply the same guard to every app-launching notification interaction.

3. **High — Deferred shield display permits app-content exposure during a blank/wake race.**  
   [powerManager.js:873](~/.local/state/rog5-gnome-mobile-src/gnome-shell/js/misc/powerManager.js:873) calls `lock(false, true)`, awaits screen-off work, then calls `showLater()`. [screenShield.js:701](~/.local/state/rog5-gnome-mobile-src/gnome-shell/js/ui/screenShield.js:701) defers both shield visibility and entering `unlock-dialog` mode.

   Screen-off work awaits the sensor daemon at `powerManager.js:635`. A second power press can cancel the blank action and call `_turnOnScreen()` at lines 365–371. While the first sensor call remains pending, the backlight-inhibited flag is still false, allowing the second operation to enable DPMS and remove the fade cover before `showLater()`. The session reports locked while the secure visual cover remains absent.

   **Fix:** Display the shield and enter lock mode synchronously before asynchronous blanking work. Serialize or generation-check power transitions so cancellation cannot remove the cover before a secure frame is presented. Test with a deliberately delayed sensor-daemon response.

4. **Medium — The mobile `Lock` D-Bus method acknowledges completion before the secure frame is shown.**  
   [shellDBus.js:534](~/.local/state/rog5-gnome-mobile-src/gnome-shell/js/ui/shellDBus.js:534) returns immediately after starting locking/blanking. Upstream 50.5 waits for `lock-screen-shown`. A caller awaiting `Lock` therefore loses the upstream completion guarantee. This matters because forced suspend at `powerManager.js:950` skips its frame wait when `screenShield.locked` is already true.  
   **Fix:** Restore the upstream completion signal contract, and make suspend wait for a presented secure frame whenever locking is still transitioning.

5. **Low — “Can only unlock through GDM” is too absolute.**  
   [screenShield.js:255](~/.local/state/rog5-gnome-mobile-src/gnome-shell/js/ui/screenShield.js:255) accepts logind `Unlock`; [shellDBus.js:541](~/.local/state/rog5-gnome-mobile-src/gnome-shell/js/ui/shellDBus.js:541) accepts session-bus `SetActive(false)`. Both reach `deactivate()`, whose authentication-prompt `finish()` merely drains messages and clears the verifier (`authPrompt.js:1073–1084`).

   These are inherited upstream trust boundaries, **not a demonstrated physical PIN bypass**. Nevertheless, they contradict README line 104’s literal claim.  
   **Fix:** Document the trusted logind/session-bus overrides. If the intended policy forbids session-bus deactivation of a locked session, restrict `SetActive(false)` separately; preserve GDM’s legitimate successful-authentication unlock path.

6. **Low — Agree: patches 0005, 0007 and 0009 address their stated problems.**  
   Evidence: [screenShield.js:430](~/.local/state/rog5-gnome-mobile-src/gnome-shell/js/ui/screenShield.js:430), [authPrompt.js:503](~/.local/state/rog5-gnome-mobile-src/gnome-shell/js/gdm/authPrompt.js:503), [powerManager.js:849](~/.local/state/rog5-gnome-mobile-src/gnome-shell/js/misc/powerManager.js:849).

   Agree that Calls overlays and Emergency activation are disabled; PIN insertion and submission respect the parental-controls block; and checking `_grab` fixes the changed `_becomeModal()` return contract. Ordinary cancel/wake paths do not independently authenticate or deactivate a locked shield.

   I found no additional Wayland application-input bypass through the visible shield or OSK. Mutter’s stage-grab handling suppresses forwarding to client windows. However, `unlockDialog.js:788` grabs `Main.uiGroup`, so its `vfunc_collect_event_actors()` should not be credited as an effective narrow event boundary for that grab.  
   **Fix:** Retain these patches and add the missing notification and transition protections above.

7. **Medium — The upstream VT-switch fix is present textually but broken functionally.**  
   [unlockDialog.js:731](~/.local/state/rog5-gnome-mobile-src/gnome-shell/js/ui/unlockDialog.js:731) waits for auth-prompt destruction before switching VT. But cancellation invokes `_fail()` at lines 502 and 659, which resets and retains the prompt. The fork’s normal destruction calls were removed/commented out.

   “Switch user” can therefore fail to switch; its queued destruction callback can instead fire later when the dialog finally dies. README coverage row 79 overstates functional coverage.  
   **Fix:** Implement a dedicated switch-user cancellation path that cancels verification, destroys the prompt after cancellation completes, and then switches VT.

8. **Low — Agree on the named escaping, username-reuse and should-lock-session ports; the string-limit protection remains ineffective.**  
   [messageList.js:594](~/.local/state/rog5-gnome-mobile-src/gnome-shell/js/ui/messageList.js:594) escapes titles; bodies pass through `Util.fixMarkup`, which permits only `b/i/u` markup (`util.js:184–201`). README’s “links” means automatically detected URLs, not accepted `<a>` markup. Username reuse at `unlockDialog.js:668–679` and the should-lock-session checks are correctly carried over, including patch 0007.

   However, title/body setters store truncated strings and still send the **original** strings to Pango and URL processing at lines 596–608. This defect is also present in upstream 50.5: the commit is merged, but its intended rendering-work bound is ineffective.  
   **Fix:** Render and scan the truncated strings, and stop grapheme iteration at the limit. Adjust the coverage table to distinguish merged code from verified behavior.

9. **High — The original Mutter pairing lacks a required Shell API; already fixed in `651eef1b`.**  
   [layout.js:344](~/.local/state/rog5-gnome-mobile-src/gnome-shell/js/ui/layout.js:344) unconditionally calls `global.display.set_keyboard_box()`. The original merged Mutter, preserved as `rog5/mobile-50.5-official`, has no corresponding API; its `src/meta/display.h` ends at line 281. The original [PKGBUILD:14](../../packages/gnome-mobile/mutter-mobile/PKGBUILD:14) selected the `c99af8f4` fork.

   That pairing raises a startup error before GNOME becomes usable, including desktop GNOME using the replaced packages.  
   **Fix:** Agree with `651eef1b`’s move to the compatible Mutter base. Add an actual Shell startup check against the packaged Mutter introspection API.

10. **High — Original fallback kills unrelated DRM clients, including logind; partly fixed in `651eef1b`.**  
    At `4717d357`, `scripts/device/rog5-shell:201–206` collects every process with the DRM device open; lines 305–311 TERM/KILL every holder. An open DRM fd does not establish compositor identity or DRM-master ownership. This can kill logind or an already-working compositor. The in-memory probe confirmed unconditional signalling of an unrelated holder.

    Agree with the newer compositor whitelist. A remaining issue is that `fallback()` ignores `stop_gnome()` returning false (`4717d357:351`) and starts Phosh anyway. A surviving compositor can retain DRM master; failure to obtain a locked Phosh then stops the display services, without an independent recovery retry.  
    **Fix:** Keep the whitelist, check release success, and distinguish ordinary DRM fds from the relevant compositor/master. Add bounded recovery retries that preserve an already-working locked Phosh.

11. **Medium — A skipped desktop GNOME start still runs destructive cleanup.**  
    [50-rog5-shell.conf:10](../../configs/systemd/rog5-gnome.service.d/50-rog5-shell.conf:10) skips desktop GNOME in mobile mode, but [rog5-gnome.service:58](../../configs/systemd/rog5-gnome.service:58) unconditionally resets shared settings and DPU performance state in `ExecStopPost`.

    A nonzero `ExecCondition` still runs `ExecStopPost`, including an ordinary condition skip. Starting the gated unit can therefore reset settings underneath the live mobile session. [systemd service documentation](https://github.com/systemd/systemd/blob/main/man/systemd.service.xml)  
    **Fix:** Run cleanup only when this unit actually performed its setup, using a setup-success marker or equivalent guarded cleanup.

12. **Medium — Watchdog login counting and readiness attribution are too broad.**  
    At `4717d357`, `rog5-shell:397–418` counts every new Shell PID as a crash-loop start. More than five within five minutes triggers fallback, including healthy greeter/user transitions. An in-memory sequence of healthy PID replacements reproduced this. `test-rog5-shell.py:400–406` tests too few, widely spaced replacements to catch it.

    Additionally, `shell_answers():250–259` reads the bus name’s property without verifying that its owner is the candidate PID. `step():403/425` accepts any answering Shell, and `phosh_alive():271–273` accepts any user Phosh process, without identifying the active phone session.

    **Fix:** Count unexpected exits/restarts per session, excluding normal login/logout transitions. Bind health checks to the active graphical session on the phone’s seat and verify the D-Bus owner PID.

13. **Medium — Emergency settings restoration inherits root’s home and XDG environment.**  
    At `4717d357`, `rog5-shell:139–142` changes UID/GID and the bus address but retains the caller’s environment. `restore_session_settings():319–328` uses this path. Yet [rog5-gnome-mobile-session:14](../../scripts/device/rog5-gnome-mobile-session:14) locates saved settings through `XDG_STATE_HOME` or `HOME`.

    Under the root watchdog, it looks in root’s state directory and silently exits without restoring the phone’s saved settings.  
    **Fix:** Supply the phone user’s `HOME`, `USER`, `LOGNAME`, runtime directory and appropriate XDG paths; remove inherited root overrides. Check restoration failures.

14. **Medium — Patch 0008’s wake classification and replay re-check leave races.**  
    Agree that [powerManager.js:524](~/.local/state/rog5-gnome-mobile-src/gnome-shell/js/misc/powerManager.js:524) and `rog5-sleep-policy:291/376` compare the same IRQ identity, and unknown wake sources retain screen-on behavior.

    However, a network/timer wake followed immediately by a real power press remains classified as “other”; fresh power presses are ignored for 500 ms at `powerManager.js:1219`. The user-active handler can consume its one-shot watch while deferring that power event to the handler that ignores it (`575–601`).

    Also, [rog5-sleep-policy:308](../../scripts/device/rog5-sleep-policy:308) checks DPMS before emitting a toggle key. Shell can enable DPMS between that read and processing the release; `powerManager.js:1200–1214` then interprets the replay as another blank.

    **Fix:** Reject stale queued events by timestamp rather than every fresh press after resume, and rearm consumed watches when ignoring input. For mobile GNOME, replace toggle replay with an acknowledged, idempotent wake operation.

15. **Medium — Subsequent installs do not revalidate the complete rollback set.**  
    [rog5-gnome-mobile-install:49](../../packages/gnome-mobile/rog5-gnome-mobile-install:49) validates archives for currently installed stock package names. After replacement, those names are absent and the loop skips them. An existing manifest bypasses regeneration at line 74; installation proceeds at line 163 even if stock archives have disappeared. `check():195–202` only prints the available files. Rollback checks file count, but does not validate every selected archive’s identity before switching sessions.

    **Fix:** Use one manifest validator before every install, check and rollback. Verify completeness and package name/version/architecture before changing packages or sessions.

    **Agree:** `--ask=4` selects `ALPM_QUESTION_CONFLICT_PKG`, appropriate for the intended replacement transaction. The first-install exact-version preservation checks are useful.

16. **Low — Agree on the basic systemd ordering, with a watchdog-liveness caveat.**  
    Agree with the one-way `After=` arrangement, the selector’s nonblocking GDM start, default-to-Phosh gates, and avoiding mutual ordering and `Conflicts=`. `ExecCondition` exit 1 skips normally; it does not fail the unit. The main-loop `ShellVersion` probe is stronger than `Peer.Ping`, and deriving credentials/bus address from the Shell process accommodates the dynamic greeter user. Session-based `Desktop`/`Class` checks are preferable to lingering user-manager environment for `gsd-power-allowed`.

    However, `Requires=` plus `After=` does not guarantee continuous watchdog liveness after spontaneous deactivation or terminal failure. [systemd unit documentation](https://github.com/systemd/systemd/blob/main/man/systemd.unit.xml)  
    **Fix:** Add an independent failure-to-fallback path and explicit restart/start-limit policy; consider `BindsTo=` with the existing ordering. Extend tests beyond command mocks to cover real `ExecCondition` cleanup and dependency behavior.

    Agree that `systemctl suspend --check-inhibitors=no` still goes through logind’s prepare-for-sleep and delay-inhibitor machinery; it does not inherently bypass Shell’s pre-suspend locking. [logind implementation](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/systemd/systemd/main/src/login/logind-dbus.c) The deferred-lock/frame-completion problems above still need fixing.
