2. **`DDR_AUX=5`: downstream blocker, root cause unverified**

   The best-supported interpretation is a DDR-related dependency keeping CX above collapse while DDR remains operational. It can therefore explain CX failure while being an **effect of the original DDR constraint**.

   Stock verifies only the label and decoding: each master contributes a byte, and an all-zero row marks LPM entry/exit. It does not document which resources DDR AUX votes, when it changes them, or the precise meaning of level `5`. Consequently, I cannot source-verify “DDR AUX directly holds MC0/SH0/ACV,” or independently confirm the report’s “nominal” interpretation. 3. **Minimal proposed experiment sequence**

   Use fresh boots and the same 60-second s2idle interval. Stop the sleep policy, verify the RTC alarm succeeded, and capture counter **deltas and residency**, not just counts. Preserve rpmhpd `sync_state`. No unknown-resource RPMh reads, display-RSC access, DSP restarts, or APR/fastrpc unbinding.

   Define a full positive result as: DDR-LPM count/residency increases, `qcom_stats/ddr` increases, and persistent DDR_AUX=5 disappears during sleep. CXSD should then increase if no independent CX holder remains; AOSD additionally requires XO release.

   | Order | Trial | Discriminating outcome |
   |---|---|---|
   | 0 | Fresh baseline with tracing and `lpm_mon` omitted | New DDR-LPM/CXSD residency implicates diagnostic interference. Persistent zeros reproduce the failure without monitoring; there is no violator prediction because logging is absent. |
   | 1 | Disable SLPI in DTB; prevent its startup in **both wrapper and inner kernel** | Full positive result implicates SLPI initialisation/sleep policy. SLPI counters should not advance. Persistent DDR_AUX=5 and zero DDR-LPM exclude SLPI as the sole blocker, provided no inherited SLPI vote remains. |
   | 2 | Boot CDSP with matching vendor firmware; leave it running | CDSP must enter sleep. Full positive result supports missing CDSP initialisation/handoff. Sleeping CDSP with unchanged floor weakens that hypothesis; continuously active COMPUTE is a confounded trial. |
   | 3 | Remove userspace battery polling, then separately trial bounded battery-notification coalescing/cache changes | Expect fewer GLINK transactions and APSS wakeups. Unchanged DDR_AUX/DDR-LPM means a wake-rate improvement only. New LPM during longer quiet intervals implicates activity/latency rather than a permanent floor. |
   | 4 | Swap only ADSP firmware between verified, matching vendor/modem variants at boot | Full positive result implicates firmware policy. An island-count increase **without DDR-LPM** shows island capability changed but did not remove the blocker. Identical results weaken variant-specific causality. |
   | 5 | Stock 5.4 wrapper capture in RAM, after separate user approval for fastboot | Stock DDR-LPM/CXSD residency establishes a usable reference. Stock zeros remain inconclusive unless DSP/client startup and actual suspend residency are demonstrated. |
