## v1

The default-to-8 change is sound for the MSI’s RGB modes, but **“every limit is enforced” is too strong**. I found these gaps; the runtime gaps predate 0147.

1. **P1 — Validation can accept more bandwidth than the actual trained link carries.** Validation and bpp selection use `panel->link_info`; `dp_max_rate` applies only during training. Training can also downshift below the required bandwidth. 0145 checks reduced-policy links, but its maximum-link fallback has no final capacity check. For example, 3840×1080@144/24 bpp remains accepted if HBR2 training falls back to HBR, which cannot carry it. Check capacity after *all* training paths and fail stream enable when insufficient. References: `dp_display.c:840`, `dp_ctrl.c:2582`, `dp_ctrl.c:2630`.

2. **P2 — TU/blanking feasibility is not an admission limit.** The bridge checks clock and aggregate payload only. TU calculation detects insufficient horizontal blanking, attempts another solution, then returns parameters without reporting failure. Thus unusual short-blanking modes can pass validation without an established valid transport configuration. Neither the 8 bpc floor nor 0145’s margin closes this. References: `dp_ctrl.c:1142`, `dp_ctrl.c:1278`.

3. **P2 — Bpp selection differs for 420-only modes.** Validation supplies `clock/2` to the bpp helper; modeset’s `msm_dp_panel_init_panel_info()` supplies the full clock. Modeset can therefore choose a lower depth than validation—even though 0145 subsequently uses that actual depth correctly. RGB modes use matching inputs. References: `dp_display.c:825`, `dp_panel.c:905`.

4. **P2 — The description overstates qualification and causality.** Rejecting the timeout-associated mode is a reasonable policy mitigation; it does not establish that 6 bpc caused the timeouts or that HBR3 makes the mode reliable. At 18 bpp, 805.12 MHz consumes only 83.87% of HBR2. Also, SSPP, multirect, resource and aggregate memory-bandwidth checks occur during atomic checking, rather than connector mode validation. Narrow the “every limit” claims in `0147:11`.

The requested positive checks:

- **MODE_BAD is correct:** with floor 24, the helper returns 24 even when it does not fit; the bridge separately rejects `805120 × 24 = 19.323 Gbit/s > 17.28`. Returning the floor is intentional. RGB modeset uses the same helper before training. `dp_panel.c:374`.
- **3840×1080@144/8 bpc is justified for continued testing:** stream clock **333.125 MHz**, mixer budget **349.781 MHz**, payload **15.99 Gbit/s**, **92.535%** of four HBR2 lanes. Its unscaled rect clocks are lower. Linear multirect rules match stock; UBWC has tighter constraints. Twenty seconds establishes brief operation, not sustained qualification.
- **Clock/voltage claims are substantially accurate:** stock halves widebus pclk against 675 MHz; the divided PHY clock is 1350 MHz at HBR/HBR2/HBR3. DPU 460 MHz requires MMCX NOM, whereas DP’s link OPP reaches NOM only at HBR3. The DPU early vote precedes encoder enable, covering the dependency. `sm8350.dtsi:2835`, `dpu_crtc.c:1313`.
- Minor wording corrections: stock computes `htotal × vtotal × integer vrefresh × 1.05 / num_lm`, so “exactly” is approximate; its 144 Hz restriction is conditional. MND ≥2 is not an additional software admission check. `stock sde_kms.c:3583`, `stock dp_panel.c:3182`.
